Whether you're new to lifting or coming back after a break, a structured plan is the fastest way to make progress. FitData makes it simple to build one that fits your goals — and track every set along the way.

## 1. Pick your focus

Start with one clear goal: strength, hypertrophy, or general fitness. This determines how many exercises, sets, and reps you'll program. A beginner full-body plan with 4–6 exercises is a great starting point.

## 2. Choose your exercises

FitData's exercise database covers compound movements like squats, bench press, and rows — plus isolation work for specific muscles. Pick 1–2 exercises per muscle group and keep the total manageable.

- Compound lifts first — they deliver the most progress per session
- Balance push and pull movements for shoulder health
- Add core work at the end of each session

## 3. Set reps, sets, and weight

For strength, aim for 3–5 sets of 4–6 reps. For muscle building, 3–4 sets of 8–12 reps works well. Log your starting weights honestly — FitData tracks personal records automatically so you always know when you've improved.

## 4. Track and adjust

Run your plan for 4–6 weeks, then review your performance charts. If you're hitting all reps easily, increase weight. If you're stalling, consider deloading or swapping an exercise. FitData's AI Coach can help adjust your plan based on your actual data.
