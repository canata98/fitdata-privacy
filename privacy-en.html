Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing in the FitData app and on fitdata.app is:

Can Ata
Hans-Böckler-Str. 176A
46242 Bottrop, Germany
Email: support@fitdata.app

This privacy policy explains which data we process, why, on which legal basis, and what rights you have. We have not appointed a data protection officer because we are not legally required to.

2. Data we process

Account

Email address, display name and sign-in method (Sign in with Apple, Google or email) via Firebase Authentication. You can also use FitData as a guest without an account; guest data stays on your device.

Training

Workout plans, exercises, sets, reps, weights, workout duration and workout streaks.

Running

Run plans, runs with GPS route, distance, pace, duration, heart rate, cadence and your rating of effort. Location is used only during a run and for the weather shown before a run.

Nutrition and body

Food diary (meals, foods, amounts, nutrients), your own foods, saved meals, favorites, weight, body fat, lean mass and BMI, and your goals.

Apple Health

Only with your permission: steps, distance, active energy, heart rate, workouts, running routes, weight and body measurements, and nutrition. If you choose, FitData writes workouts, runs, weight and nutrition back to Apple Health.

AI chat and AI features

Your messages to the AI assistant, its answers and the context it needs from your training, running and nutrition data. Chats are saved in your account so you can reopen them later, until you delete them or your account.

Meal photos

Photos you send to recognize a meal. A photo is transmitted only for that single analysis and is not stored. We store only how many photos you used in a month.

Barcodes

Scanned barcodes, to look up product and nutrition data.

Subscription

Apple purchase receipts (product, term, transaction ID) so we can verify your FitData Pro subscription. We never receive payment details; Apple handles payment.

Usage and diagnostics

Anonymous usage statistics, crash reports and app performance data (app version, device model, OS version). You can turn analytics and crash reporting off at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy.

Website

Page views and App Store clicks on fitdata.app, only if you agree in the cookie banner.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the app (account, storing and syncing your data, training, running and nutrition features, AI features, subscription checks): performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Health data (Apple Health, heart rate, weight, body and nutrition data): your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, given when you grant access in Apple Health or enter it in FitData, and revocable at any time.
  • Location for runs and weather: your consent through iOS location permission (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Crash reports, performance data, app statistics and abuse protection: our legitimate interest in a stable and secure app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can turn analytics and crash reporting off in the app.
  • Website analytics: your consent in the banner (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG).

We do not use your data for advertising, build no advertising profiles and never sell data. App suggestions in "Discover apps" are chosen app to app, never based on you.

4. Recipients and services

Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC)

Sign-in, database (Cloud Firestore), server functions, file storage for exercise images, App Check (abuse protection), Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring. Our server functions run in Frankfurt (europe-west3).

Google Gemini (via Firebase Cloud Functions)

Answers AI chat messages, recognizes meals in photos and text, and looks up nutrition values of branded and restaurant products (this may use Google Search). We send your message or photo and the context needed, not your email address.

Apple Intelligence

Simple AI tasks, such as chat titles or short corrections to your meals, run on Apple's language model on your device or via Apple's Private Cloud Compute.

Apple

Apple Health (see above), Sign in with Apple, the App Store for purchases, WeatherKit for the weather shown before a run (your approximate location is sent to Apple for this) and Apple Maps for running routes.

Google Sign-In

Only if you sign in with Google.

Open Food Facts

When you scan a barcode, we query the open product database openfoodfacts.org with that barcode. No data about you is sent.

Kickstart Exchange (Hacking with Swift)

Provides the app suggestions in "Discover apps". The service receives only app data such as bundle ID, app and build version, platform and App Store country, never a user or device identifier. See the Kickstart Exchange privacy notice.

Google Analytics 4 (website)

Loaded on fitdata.app only if you agree in the banner.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Google and Apple may also process data in the United States. Both are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition.

6. Health data

We use health, body and nutrition data only to show you your activity, your progress and AI answers. We never share it for advertising and never sell it. For AI features, only the values needed for that specific answer are sent to Gemini.

7. Storage and security

Your data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and also on your device, so FitData works offline. Transfers are encrypted with TLS, and stored data is encrypted at rest. Only your own account can access your data.

We keep your data as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app (Settings → Profile → Delete Account), all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days. Firebase deletes crash reports after at most 90 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we store about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16) and erasure (Art. 17), also directly in the app
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), for example via CSV export in the app
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw your consent for the future, for example for Apple Health and location in iOS Settings
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia

To exercise these rights, email support@fitdata.app.

9. Children

FitData is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 16, use FitData only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian where the law requires it. If a child has sent us data, please contact us.

10. Changes

When the app or the law changes, we update this policy. We tell you about material changes in the app or by email. For new processing that requires consent, we ask you first.
