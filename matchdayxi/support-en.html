Common questions

What is Matchday XI?

A fantasy football manager for private leagues. Build a squad, trade with friends and set your XI before kickoff. Points come from real football matches.

How do I play with friends?

Create a private league or join using an invitation code. Depending on the league, an admin may need to approve your join request.

How do points and budgets work?

Real match events and ratings contribute to points. Each league sets its rules. Bids reserve budget; winning bids, transfers and matchday prizes are settled on the server.

Can I use Matchday XI offline?

Some previously loaded content may remain available. Sign-in, league changes, bids and current sports data require a connection. Fully offline play is not guaranteed.

What happens when I delete my account?

Your account, profile and stored push tokens are removed. Leagues you own are deleted; other leagues remain. Historical transactions, matchday data and technical user IDs may remain. The privacy page explains these limits.

Is the app on the App Store yet?

The first public release is being prepared. The official App Store link will appear here once the app is available.

Troubleshooting

Cannot sign in

Check your connection and use the same sign-in provider as before. Restart the app. Never share passwords or codes with support.

Missing league or invitation

Check the selected account and invitation code. A join request may be waiting for admin approval.

Bid or points look outdated

Check your connection and refresh the view. Pending bids reserve budget; sports data and settlement may arrive with a delay. Do not send confidential sign-in information to support.

Missing notifications

Check preferences in Matchday XI and under iOS → Settings → Notifications.

Delete account

Open Profile → Account → Delete account. If deletion fails, check the message and contact us. The privacy policy explains which historical records may remain.

Contact

Questions about the app or your data? Contact us with your app version and device. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
