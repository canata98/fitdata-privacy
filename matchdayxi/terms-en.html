Updated: October 16, 2026

1. What Matchday XI is for

Matchday XI lets you play fantasy football in private leagues and design your own teams in Squad Builder. The provider is Can Ata; contact details are in the imprint. These terms apply alongside the app’s functions and the relevant league settings.

2. Your access

Use your own accurate account information and protect access to your account. Do not share passwords or sign-in codes. Certain functions require an account and an internet connection.

3. Play fairly together

League owners and authorized admins manage invitations and league settings. Follow the agreed rules. Manipulation, abusive automation, unauthorized access and exploitation of technical errors are not permitted.

4. Transfers and scoring

Bids, transfers and settlement follow the relevant league settings. Budgets, player values and matchday prizes are virtual game values and do not create an entitlement to real-money payouts. Sports data, ratings and scores may be delayed, incomplete or incorrect and may be corrected later. Previously settled points of former members remain in historical standings. Leaving a league does not in itself redistribute other members’ prizes.

5. Content and rights

Use the app for its intended purpose. Only upload or share content you are entitled to use. Rights to club, player and sports data remain with the respective rights holders. This website does not claim an official partnership with clubs or competitions.

6. Availability

Maintenance, connection problems or external service outages may temporarily limit functionality. Accounts used abusively may be suspended. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

7. Delete your account or get help

The privacy policy explains account deletion and data that may remain. For help using the app, contact support.
