Privacy policy

Updated: October 15, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

Can Ata is responsible for Matchday XI. The address and other provider details are in the imprint. For questions about your data, email support@canata.app.

2. Your account and league

We use Firebase Authentication for sign-in. Depending on the method you choose, we process your account ID, email address, display name and sign-in provider information. Firestore stores the data needed to play together: leagues and memberships, roles and invitations, squads, lineups, bids, offers, transactions and matchday results. Other league members can see the data intended for shared gameplay.

3. Data on your device

The app also stores account data, previously loaded content and your Squad Builder lineups locally. Sign-in, bids and current sports data require an internet connection. After successful account deletion, the associated local account data and builds are removed.

4. Notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token to send updates about matters such as bids and matchdays. You choose which notifications to receive in the app and in iOS Settings. Account deletion removes the push tokens stored under your user profile.

5. Crash reports and usage data

Firebase Crashlytics helps us investigate crashes. Reports may be linked to your user ID. Firebase Analytics processes usage events to help us understand and improve the app. This processing is not fully anonymous.

6. Services we use

We use Firebase and Google services for sign-in, the database, server processing, push notifications, crash reports and usage analytics. Apple or Google may also be involved depending on your sign-in method. Football data comes from the BSD Sports API. The Matchday section of this website currently includes no website analytics. Fonts are served directly by the website.

7. Deleting your account

Start deletion under Profile → Account → Delete account. Your Matchday account, profile and stored push tokens are removed. Leagues you own are deleted. You leave other leagues, which remain available to their other members. Attributed contributions and display names are selectively removed. However, historical transactions, matchday data and technical user IDs may remain. Older contributions without clear attribution, or data in leagues no longer associated with your account, may also remain. Account deletion therefore does not remove every historical link. Analytics data and crash reports already collected are not separately deleted by this process. Complete deletion of all history within a fixed period is not guaranteed.

8. Questions and rights concerning your data

You can contact us about access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of processing. Depending on the processing involved, you may also object or withdraw consent. You can also complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority. We do not sell personal data.

9. Updates to this information

We update this information when the app or its data processing changes. The date at the top identifies the version you are reading.
