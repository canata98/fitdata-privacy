Privacy policy

Updated: October 15, 2026

1. Controller and contact

Can Ata is responsible for Matchday XI. The provider address is listed in the imprint. For privacy requests, contact support@canata.app.

2. Account and league data

Matchday XI uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in. Depending on the sign-in method, your account ID, email address, display name and sign-in provider information are processed. Firestore stores leagues, memberships, roles, invitations, squads, lineups, bids, offers, budget transactions and matchday data. Other league members can see data intended for shared gameplay.

3. Local data and connectivity

The app keeps local account data, cached content and saved Squad Builder lineups. Sign-in, server actions and current sports data require a connection. After successful account deletion, the associated local account data and builds are cleared.

4. Notifications

When notifications are enabled, device push tokens are stored to notify you about matters such as bids and matchdays. You can change notification preferences in the app and in iOS Settings. Account deletion removes push tokens stored under your user profile.

5. Diagnostics and usage events

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports for error diagnosis. Reports may be linked to your user ID. Firebase Analytics processes usage events. Complete anonymity is not guaranteed. New deep-link events contain fixed route categories rather than complete invitation or build URLs; this does not remove data previously collected.

6. Service providers and sports data

Firebase and Google services are used for sign-in, the database, server processing, notifications, diagnostics and usage analytics. Apple and Google may be involved depending on your chosen sign-in method. Sports data comes from the BSD Sports API. The website uses locally hosted fonts; the Matchday section currently includes no website analytics. Technical processing by the future hosting provider must be reviewed separately before publication.

7. Account deletion and remaining history

You can start deletion under Profile → Account → Delete account. Your authentication account, profile and stored push tokens are removed. Leagues you own are deleted; other leagues remain and you leave them. Attributed contributions and display names are selectively removed. Historical transactions, matchday data and technical user IDs may remain in other leagues; older contributions without clear attribution or data from leagues no longer associated with your account may remain. Account deletion does not separately delete Analytics data or crash reports already collected. Complete deletion of all history within a fixed period is not guaranteed.

8. Your privacy requests

You can contact us about access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of processing, and raise an objection or withdraw consent where applicable. The rights that apply depend on the processing involved. You can also contact a competent data protection supervisory authority. We do not sell personal data.

9. Changes

This information is updated when the app or its data processing changes. The published version applicable at the time governs.

Contact

Questions about the app or your data? Contact us with your app version and device. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
