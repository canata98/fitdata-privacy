Updated: October 15, 2026

1. The app

Matchday XI is a fantasy football manager for private leagues and a Squad Builder. The app functions, actual league settings and usage rules described here form the basis for using the service.

2. Account and access

Use your own accurate account information and protect access to your account. Do not share passwords or sign-in codes. Some functions require sign-in and a connection to the backend.

3. Private leagues and fair play

League owners and authorized admins manage invitations and league settings. Follow the agreed rules. Manipulation, abusive automation, unauthorized access and exploitation of technical errors are not permitted.

4. Market, points and data

Bids, transfers and settlement follow the league settings applicable to the relevant action. Sports data, ratings and scores may be delayed, incomplete or incorrect and may be corrected later. Previously settled points of former members remain in historical standings. Leaving a league does not in itself redistribute other members’ prizes.

5. Content and rights

Use the app only for its intended purpose. Upload or share only content you are authorized to use. Rights to actual club, player and sports data remain with the respective rights holders; this website does not claim an official partnership with clubs or competitions.

6. Availability and abuse

We work to provide a reliable service. Maintenance, connectivity issues and external service outages may limit functionality. Accounts used abusively may be suspended. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.

7. Account and privacy

See the privacy policy for account deletion and remaining data. Contact support if you have questions or problems.
