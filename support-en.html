FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

What can I use offline?
Saved plans, workout logging, cached food search, and your local diary are available offline. Synchronization, cloud AI, and uncached product lookups need a connection.
What's included in FitData Pro?
Pro adds a larger monthly AI allowance and premium planning and analysis features. Meal photo recognition is a Pro feature with a limited free trial. See the subscription screen in the app for current limits and offers.
Can I track food and weight?
Yes. Log foods and meals, review calories and macros, set nutrition targets, and follow your weight history. AI suggestions are reviewed before saving.
How does Apple Health integration work?
With your permission, FitData reads supported activity and health data and saves workouts and nutrition entries to Apple Health. Available data depends on your permissions and recording devices.
How does AI processing work?
Cloud chat and meal photo recognition use Google Gemini and require a connection. Some supported tasks run with Apple Intelligence when available. HealthKit measurements stay out of Gemini requests. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Does FitData support iPhone Duo?
Yes. FitData 3.4 adapts its dashboards to the inner display, with related cards shown side by side. The video above shows the app running in the iPhone Duo simulator.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Manage or cancel your Pro subscription in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

AI Chat Issues

  • Check your monthly conversation limit
